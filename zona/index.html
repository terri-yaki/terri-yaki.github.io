Zona Notify Support

Get help with sources, alerts, accounts, devices, or Zona Plus.

Contact

Email terabasedevteam@gmail.com with your Zona version, device model, and a clear description of what happened. Never send a complete source token, password, push token, payment information, or real alert content.

Before contacting us

Open Settings → App Status and copy the safe diagnostics. Check that notification permission is enabled and that the alert appears in your inbox. An inbox record may exist even when a push banner does not appear.

Subscriptions

After reinstalling or changing devices, use Settings → Zona Plus → Restore Purchases. Use Manage Subscription to change or cancel through Apple. Apple handles charges and refunds.

Zona Notify 支援

如需來源、通知、帳戶、裝置或 Zona Plus 方面的協助，請參閱以下資料。

聯絡我們

請電郵至 terabasedevteam@gmail.com，並提供 Zona 版本、裝置型號及清楚的問題描述；請勿傳送完整來源密鑰、密碼、推送通知識別碼、付款資料或真實通知內容。

聯絡前可先檢查

開啟「設定 → App 狀態」並複製可安全分享的診斷資料，然後檢查系統已允許通知，以及通知是否已出現在收件匣；即使沒有顯示推送橫額，通知仍可能已儲存在收件匣。

訂閱

重新安裝或更換裝置後，請前往「設定 → Zona Plus → 還原購買項目」；你亦可透過「管理訂閱項目」在 Apple 更改或取消訂閱，收費及退款由 Apple 處理。
