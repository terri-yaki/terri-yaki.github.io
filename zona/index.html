Zona Notify Support

Need help connecting a source, receiving an alert, protecting your account, or managing Zona Plus?

Contact

Email terabasedevteam@gmail.com with your Zona version, device model, and a description of what happened. Never send a complete source token, password, push token, or payment information.

Subscriptions

Use Settings → Zona Plus → Restore Purchases after reinstalling or changing phones. Use Manage Subscription to change or cancel through Apple. Apple handles charges and refunds.

Privacy

Read the Zona Notify Privacy Policy.

Zona Notify 支援

如需協助連接來源、接收通知、保護帳戶或管理 Zona Plus，請電郵至 terabasedevteam@gmail.com，並提供 Zona 版本、裝置型號及問題描述。請勿傳送完整來源權杖、密碼、推播權杖或付款資料。

訂閱

重新安裝或更換手機後，可前往「設定 → Zona Plus → 回復購買項目」。你亦可透過「管理訂閱項目」在 Apple 更改或取消訂閱；收費及退款由 Apple 處理。

私隱

閱讀 Zona Notify 私隱政策。
