Zona Notify Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

What Zona stores

Zona stores account identifiers and sign-in details, source names and optional hostnames, notification content and attachments, phone installation and push-delivery identifiers, service diagnostics, and your notification preferences.

If you subscribe to Zona Plus, Zona stores the App Store product identifier, original and current transaction identifiers, subscription status, environment, and expiry. These records are linked to your Zona account so purchases can be verified, restored, renewed, expired, refunded, or revoked. Zona never receives your payment-card details.

How the data is used

Data is used only to provide, secure, support, and improve Zona: receiving and synchronizing alerts, delivering push notifications, enforcing account limits, verifying subscriptions, restoring purchases, preventing abuse, and diagnosing failures. Zona does not sell personal data, serve behavioral advertising, or track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Services involved

Zona uses Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and server functions; Expo and Apple Push Notification service for iPhone delivery; Firebase Cloud Messaging for Android delivery when configured; Apple for App Store purchases; and the sign-in provider you choose. Each provider processes the minimum information needed for its role.

Retention and control

Standard inbox items are normally retained for seven days; Plus limits may provide longer history. Attachments follow their notification. Security, delivery, and subscription records are retained only as needed to operate the service, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. You can delete individual notifications, revoke sources, sign out, or permanently delete your Zona account and associated Zona data from Settings. Apple may retain purchase records under its own policies.

Zona Notify 私隱政策

最後更新：2026 年 8 月 24 日

Zona 儲存的資料

Zona 會儲存帳戶及登入識別資料、來源名稱及選填主機名稱、通知內容及附件、手機安裝及推播識別資料、服務診斷資料，以及你的通知偏好。

如你訂閱 Zona Plus，Zona 亦會儲存 App Store 產品識別碼、原始及目前交易識別碼、訂閱狀態、環境及到期時間，並與 Zona 帳戶連結，以核實及回復購買、處理續訂、到期、退款或撤銷。Zona 不會收到你的付款卡資料。

資料用途

資料只會用於提供、保障、支援及改善 Zona，包括接收及同步通知、傳送推播、執行帳戶限制、核實訂閱、回復購買、防止濫用及診斷故障。Zona 不會出售個人資料、提供行為廣告，或跨其他公司的 App 或網站追蹤你。

相關服務

Zona 使用 Supabase 提供登入、資料庫、儲存及伺服器功能；使用 Expo 及 Apple Push Notification service 傳送 iPhone 通知；在已設定時使用 Firebase Cloud Messaging 傳送 Android 通知；使用 Apple 處理 App Store 購買；以及使用你選擇的登入供應商。各供應商只會處理履行其用途所需的資料。

保留及控制

標準方案的收件匣內容一般保留七天，Plus 方案可提供較長紀錄；附件會跟隨所屬通知。保安、傳送及訂閱紀錄只會在營運服務、處理爭議、防止詐騙及履行法律責任所需期間保留。你可刪除個別通知、撤銷來源、登出，或在「設定」永久刪除 Zona 帳戶及相關 Zona 資料。Apple 可能按其政策保留購買紀錄。
