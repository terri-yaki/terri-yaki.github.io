Zona Notify Privacy Policy

Effective 29 August 2026 · Operator: Ho Yu Lee (Terabase) · Hong Kong

What Zona does

Zona provides a synchronized inbox for alerts sent by sources you configure. Zona stores an accepted alert before requesting eligible push delivery. Push delivery is best effort and does not prove that a notification appeared on a phone.

Data Zona processes

Zona processes account identifiers and sign-in details; source names, optional hostnames, and one-way hashes of source keys; notification content, categories, timestamps, and optional image attachments; installation identifiers, push-notification identifiers, preferences, delivery results, and service diagnostics.

If you use Zona Plus, Zona also stores the App Store product identifier, original and current transaction identifiers, subscription status, environment, and expiry date. These records are linked to your Zona account so Zona can verify subscriptions and restore purchases. Zona never receives your payment-card details.

How Zona uses data

Zona uses this data to authenticate accounts, isolate each user’s data, receive and synchronize alerts, attempt push delivery, enforce limits, restore purchases, prevent abuse, respond to support requests, and diagnose or secure the service. Zona does not sell personal data, serve behavioural advertising, or track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Notification content

Notification titles, bodies, source labels, and selected routing details pass through Supabase, Expo, and Apple or Google push services. They may appear on a lock screen or to someone with access to your device. Do not send passwords, source keys, personal access tokens, sign-in links, payment details, or unnecessary sensitive information in an alert.

Service providers and international processing

Zona uses Supabase for authentication, database, storage, logs, and server functions; Expo and Apple Push Notification service for iPhone delivery; Firebase Cloud Messaging for Android delivery when configured; Apple for App Store purchases; and the sign-in provider you choose. These providers may process data outside Hong Kong under their own terms and safeguards, subject to applicable law.

Retention

Current default inbox retention is one day for guest accounts, seven days for Standard accounts, and thirty days for Zona Plus. The period shown in Settings is authoritative and attachments follow their notification. Rate-limit rows are normally kept for one day. Account, source, device, security, and subscription records are kept only as needed to provide and protect the service, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or meet legal obligations. Apple retains its own purchase records under Apple’s policies.

Your choices and rights

You may use the inbox without granting notification permission. In Zona you can revoke sources, delete individual notifications, sign out devices, control lock-screen previews, and permanently delete your Zona account and associated app data. Deleting your Zona account does not cancel an App Store subscription.

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection where applicable. Zona may need to verify that a request belongs to the account before acting.

Security, children, and changes

Zona uses TLS, Supabase Auth, row-level security, hashed source keys, payload and rate limits, independent revocation, and bounded retention. No service is perfectly secure. Zona is not directed to children.

Material changes to data collection, purpose, providers, retention, or user rights will require an updated notice and any notice or consent required by law.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: terabasedevteam@gmail.com. Never email a password, complete source key, push token, payment information, or real alert content.

Zona Notify 私隱政策

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 29 日 · 營運者：Ho Yu Lee（Terabase）· 香港

Zona 提供的服務

Zona 為你設定的來源提供同步通知收件匣，通知獲接受後會先儲存，再按情況要求推送通知服務傳送；推送通知屬盡力傳送，並不代表通知一定已在手機顯示。

Zona 處理的資料

Zona 會處理帳戶識別碼及登入資料；來源名稱、選填的主機名稱及來源密鑰的單向雜湊值；通知內容、類別、時間及選填圖片附件；以及安裝識別碼、推送通知識別碼、偏好設定、傳送結果及服務診斷資料。

如你使用 Zona Plus，Zona 亦會儲存 App Store 產品識別碼、原始及目前交易識別碼、訂閱狀態、環境及到期日，並將記錄連結至你的 Zona 帳戶，以核實訂閱及還原購買項目；Zona 不會取得你的付款卡資料。

資料用途

Zona 會使用資料驗證帳戶、分隔每名用戶的資料、接收及同步通知、嘗試傳送推送通知、執行用量限制、還原購買項目、防止濫用、處理支援要求，以及診斷或保障服務；Zona 不會出售個人資料、提供行為廣告，或跨其他公司的 App 或網站追蹤你。

通知內容

通知標題、內容、來源標籤及部分傳送資料會經 Supabase、Expo 以及 Apple 或 Google 的推送通知服務處理，並可能顯示在鎖定畫面，或被能夠使用你裝置的人看到；請勿在通知中加入密碼、來源密鑰、個人存取權杖、登入連結、付款資料或不必要的敏感資料。

服務供應商及跨境處理

Zona 使用 Supabase 提供登入、資料庫、儲存、記錄及伺服器功能；使用 Expo 及 Apple Push Notification service 傳送 iPhone 通知；在已設定時使用 Firebase Cloud Messaging 傳送 Android 通知；使用 Apple 處理 App Store 購買；以及使用你選擇的登入服務；這些供應商可能按其條款及保障措施在香港以外處理資料，並須遵守適用法律。

資料保留

現時預設的收件匣保留期為：訪客帳戶一天、標準帳戶七天、Zona Plus 三十天；實際保留期以「設定」所示為準，附件會跟隨所屬通知刪除；用量限制記錄一般保留一天，帳戶、來源、裝置、保安及訂閱記錄則只會在提供及保障服務、處理爭議、防止詐騙或履行法律責任所需期間保留；Apple 會按其政策保留本身的購買記錄。

你的選擇及權利

即使不允許推送通知，你仍可使用收件匣；你可在 Zona 撤銷來源、刪除個別通知、登出裝置、管理鎖定畫面預覽，以及永久刪除 Zona 帳戶及相關 App 資料；刪除 Zona 帳戶不會取消 App Store 訂閱。

在適用情況下，你可要求查閱、更正、刪除、限制或轉移資料，或提出反對；Zona 在處理要求前可能需要核實要求屬於有關帳戶。

保安、兒童及政策更新

Zona 使用 TLS、Supabase Auth、資料列層級保安、來源密鑰雜湊、內容及用量限制、獨立撤銷及有限保留期保障服務；任何服務均無法保證絕對安全，Zona 並非為兒童而設。

如資料收集、用途、供應商、保留期或用戶權利有重大變更，Zona 會更新本政策，並按法律要求提供通知或徵求同意。
